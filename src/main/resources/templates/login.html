Student services

Your college account covers the following, all from this portal.

  • Open your account with your name, email, phone number, address, department, gender, interests and date of birth.
  • Review your account details at any time on your profile page.
  • Update your name, department, phone number, address and preferences; the change is stored in the database straight away.
  • Recover access with a local demonstration reset token if you forget your password.

Sign in details are checked on the server. Passwords are stored only as salted PBKDF2 hashes.
